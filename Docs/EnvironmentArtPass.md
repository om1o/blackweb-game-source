# Environment art pass — 5 November 2026

This source pass adds distinct blood and black-residue masks, physically varied material responses, and three original strand-mesh web shapes to the five-room tutorial house. Native captures of all five rooms have been reviewed under production lighting: the materials and strand placements are legible, and the logs contain no shader errors. The final source-runtime route completed all nine legs through all five rooms at about 41 FPS average. This is a bounded source-build review, not a claim of AAA quality or a complete human playtest.

The eleven distinct texture payloads actively referenced by `Godot/scripts/environment_surfaces.gd` total **95,030,515 bytes**, or **95.03 decimal MB**. This exceeds the requested 90 MB of dedicated surface artwork. The count comes from `EnvironmentArtManifest.json`; every recorded size and SHA-256 was checked against the actual file, and all eleven hashes are unique. It excludes source ZIP archives, alternate normal conventions, duplicate files, previews, previous art, imported caches and the new web GLB. It is a source-file storage count, not a claim about GPU memory use, visual quality, or compressed package size.

The nine sourced PBR maps retain their 4096-pixel originals. The two original generated atlases are 1254 pixels square; they are not described as 4K. All eleven import configurations enable mipmaps and impose a maximum dimension of 2048. That cap preserves smaller images rather than enlarging them. The three normal maps now import at 2048 pixels using RGTC compression with mipmaps, with a stored imported-image payload of **5,592,432 bytes each**. The environment shaders reconstruct the normal's Z component from the compressed RG channels. Detail UV coordinates on residue are based on surface dimensions in metres rather than stretched to each atlas quadrant. All eleven `.import` configurations are tracked through explicit `.gitignore` exceptions, so a clone retains these import choices.

`Evidence/environment-imported-textures.json` passed its format, dimension and mipmap checks. Its byte values describe stored imported-image payloads, not whole-process VRAM or frame performance. The source artwork remains 95,030,515 bytes. The later route comparison is reported separately from this storage measurement.

The existing **150 residue placements** now share three material surfaces: dry blood, wet wall runoff and pooled floor residue. Four blood masks and four residue masks provide **eight image variants**. Blood remains rough and subdued; the two residue materials use different normal and roughness maps to change their flashlight response. The placements are batched into three mesh surfaces rather than creating a material and node for every stain. These stains are surface-aligned cards with alpha cutouts, not volume meshes or simulated fluids.
