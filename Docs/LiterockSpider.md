# Visible tutorial spider

The tutorial now uses [Giant Spider Lite by Literock Studios](https://literock-studios.itch.io/giant-spider-lite) for the small supply-room and vault spiders. The licensed GLB has one skinned mesh, a 46-bone imported skeleton, and authored idle, walk, run, and bite clips. Godot selects bite during the 0.28-second attack tell; fatal hits use a short procedural fall because the free asset has no death animation. The combat juvenile has 12 base health, no armor, a 1.35 m/s walk speed, a 1.6-second bite cooldown, and 4 damage in this tutorial. The flashlight slows it by up to 20 percent for 0.42 seconds.

The source 2K GLB is stored locally under `RecoveredAssets/LicensedThirdParty/Literock/Giant_Spider_Lite_original_2K.glb`. The playable project uses `Godot/assets/third_party/literock/Giant_Spider_Lite_1K.glb`, made with `Tools/AssetImport/downscale_glb_textures.py`; that reduces the source GLB from 16.37 MB to 4.47 MB while leaving its mesh, rig, and animation buffers unchanged. Its three embedded maps and extracted Godot images are 1024 px. The supplied license permits the asset inside a compiled game but forbids redistributing the raw asset or a modified asset pack, so the entire Literock directory is excluded from Git. A source checkout without it uses the CC0 Quaternius spider as a functional fallback.

The first harmless spider is positioned in front of the supply-room couch and visible through its open doorway. `Godot/tests/spider_sightline_tests.gd` checks the doorway ray; `Godot/tests/juvenile_visual_tests.gd` checks the model and animations. Native captures are in the local `Evidence/spider-literock-doorway.png`, `Evidence/spider-literock-house-fill.png`, and `Evidence/spider-literock-bite.png` files. The 1K and 2K renders showed no material difference at gameplay distance in that view. On the Intel HD 530, the isolated visible/hidden probe added two draw calls and roughly 2–3 ms median frame time when one spider was in view; texture memory in the sampled scene fell from about 435 MB with the 2K model to about 360 MB with the 1K model and unused 2K files removed. These short samples are a local comparison, not a guaranteed FPS improvement.
