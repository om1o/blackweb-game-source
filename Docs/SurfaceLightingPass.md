# Compact-house surface and prop finish pass

The five-room tutorial now keeps its web and stain dressing inside the cropped shell. The four interior doorways have brick lintels from 2.78 to 3.20 m, with UV scale adjusted so the bricks are not squashed. Physical probes measured the floor at y=0.40 and ceiling underside at y=3.20. Sixteen floor stains previously sat at y=0.026, below the boards; they now sit at y=0.414.

Cross-wall stains now have X-facing normals, and spine stains/silk sit outside both faces of the 0.20 m wall. Texture V is corrected so drips run down. Lit card winding matches Godot's clockwise front faces. Corner, radial, hanging and furniture silk now carry normals and respond to the room/flashlight lighting instead of remaining bright in darkness. The pass keeps 150 small residue cards, 112 dense silk cards, seven corner fans, five radial webs, five veils, five ceiling bridges and six furniture wraps. Source art is reused rather than duplicated to pad a file-size target.

Native `Evidence/tutorial-entry-native.png` and `tutorial-rear-spider-native.png` were inspected in the Intel HD 530 ANGLE renderer. The corrected ceiling, walls, stains and silk are visible. This remains a prototype: silhouette geometry, repeated dressing, low-end lighting, flat paper/cash props and synthetic voice delivery still limit realism. There is no claim of AAA fidelity or a 100-fold performance improvement.

## New original Imagegen surface

Built-in Imagegen generated `Godot/assets/generated/blood_goo_smears_v2.png`, 1,829,667 bytes, SHA-256 `603E309EB3EB20E9DF6ECDF5F6C80A95047C9FAA984B7D5615900F3C6088B149`. A first result contained a pale substrate and was rejected; a targeted transparency edit produced the integrated asset. The original v1 remains available. No API/CLI fallback or image post-processing was used.

Generation prompt: "Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: in-game transparent PBR-style color decal atlas for a realistic abandoned-house horror game. Create ONE wide 2:1 PNG atlas, genuinely transparent background, exactly two equally sized square regions with generous transparent padding and NO dividing lines. LEFT HALF: irregular dark dried human-blood residue on an invisible vertical surface, broad thin smeared contact stain with tiny fine splashes and a few gravity-down thin rivulets. Deep desaturated oxblood and brown, varied translucent rubbed edges, dried grain and flaking, no flesh or body parts. RIGHT HALF: irregular thin layer of black organic oily resin on an invisible vertical surface, rubbed sideways at upper area then fine tapering downward drips; subtle dark charcoal/green-brown variations, sparse tendrils. Both must read as SURFACE STAINS, almost flat and spread out, NOT a thick floating 3D blob or sculpture. Orthographic straight-on view, neutral diffuse color only; no cast shadow, no bright baked highlights, no glossy white shine. Edge transparency with tiny separated speckles, no background wall or floor, no black rectangle, no checkerboard baked in, no text, no frame, no logos. Full subject inside each half with 8% margins. Downward drips toward the bottom of the image. Crisp physically plausible photographic texture detail suitable for lit decals at realistic scale."

Final edit prompt: "Edit target: this two-half horror game decal atlas. Change only background extraction: REMOVE ALL white/gray/beige/pale substrate and haze from both halves, including the pale area beneath every stain, completely replace it with ACTUAL TRANSPARENCY. Keep only the dark brown-red blood smears and splashes on the left and charcoal-black oily residue on the right, preserving their microscopic details and downward drip shapes. The asset must be usable over arbitrary colored brick, so no white material/paint/paper remains and no glow, bright outline or filled rectangle. Sparse truly transparent gaps between speckles. Preserve exact two-halves layout, dimensions and scale. Genuinely transparent PNG alpha, not a drawn checkerboard or solid background."
