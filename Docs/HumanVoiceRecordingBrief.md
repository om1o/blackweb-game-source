# Blackweb — human dialogue replacement brief

This is a recording brief, not a completed voice replacement. Current shipped dialogue remains synthetic pending the user's choice. No actor has been contacted or commissioned, and no person's voice has been cloned.

Record natural English at 48 kHz, mono WAV, without music, radio filtering or reverb. Keep breaths and pauses that serve the performance. Leave a short clean handle at each end. Deliver one coherent take per line below; post-production will handle the phone disconnect and subtitle timing. Avoid an announcer delivery. Mica is frightened and trying to stay quiet; Luke is tired from work, concerned, then increasingly alarmed.

The final word of the phone take should remain intelligible before the connection cuts. Do not rush the rest of the message to meet the current synthetic recording's duration. Replace the corresponding assets, measure the new takes, update `phone_dialogue.json`, `story_voice.json` and cinematic timing, then repeat audio capture, subtitle-sync and intro playback checks.
