# Blackweb gameplay milestone — source verification

This document records the earlier gameplay milestone. For the current five-room build, use [TutorialHousePass.md](TutorialHousePass.md), [SupplyActionPass.md](SupplyActionPass.md) and [PhoneVoicePass.md](PhoneVoicePass.md). Supplies now use original Blender meshes and delayed use events, Mara and Luke's phone exchange is voiced, and newer evidence supersedes the historical counts below. The old narrative route did not establish access to every optional room; the new full-capsule traversal regression covers all five rooms.

The active Godot source supports hold-`C` crouching with a shorter collision capsule and a clearance check before standing, `Space` jumping, and `Shift` sprinting that spends and recovers stamina. Jumping costs nine stamina. The existing phone, warning, house pickup, encounter, exit, and Greyhaven route still works.

Luke starts with a working flashlight at 22% charge, a low-damage worn knife, and one candy bar. The flashlight works before the phone and before the later charged-backup pickup. It drains 0.22 percentage points per second while on, flickers more often below 8%, and cuts out at zero. The backup pickup strengthens the beam while preserving its story gate. The 1K CC0 Poly Haven flashlight model is attached to Luke's recovered right-hand skeleton, with a close-camera fallback. Beam energy and specular output have been reduced for pale furnishings; the native held-pose captures were reviewed for this prototype.

`1`–`3` use the three equipped items. They begin as flashlight, weak worn knife, and candy bar; `F` also toggles the flashlight while it is equipped. A knife stab applies its small damage at the hit window, and eating candy restores 30 health at the bite timing. Bandages heal 55 and flares light for ten seconds, but must replace one of the three quick items before use. The flashlight and knife use imported CC0 models, while other hand items use code-native models. Timed procedural poses and short original synthesized Foley cues are in source for these actions and spider attacks. Headless tests verify item timing and effects. These remain procedural action performances rather than final authored animation and audio.

The chosen **Minimal** HUD is the default. It has transparent, labeled health/stamina/battery meters in the lower-left corner and three item indicators in the lower-right corner. The battery shows its percentage. `F1`–`F4` still choose Field, Survival, Minimal, or Vertical. `I` opens an interactive 50-cell inventory that pauses combat and frees the mouse. Click an occupied backpack cell, then one of three quick-slot buttons to equip it; `1`–`3` use those active items after closing. The same overlay shows loadout, supplies, condition, and records. Its 50 cells are the actual item stacks, with up to ten common consumables or five battery packs of the same charge per cell. No fourth or fifth use slot exists.

Two reachable fixtures in the opening house follow `Open → Search → Close`: EntryDrawer slides and HallCabinet swings. Three guaranteed world pickups include battery cells, one bandage, and one flare. The world battery is stored as a +35% pack and the drawer battery as a +30% pack; neither charges the flashlight until equipped in a quick slot and used. Packs can be carried even when the torch is full. The drawer also has a 35% extra-bandage roll; the cabinet always has a bandage and has a 40% extra-flare roll. Contents are seeded once per new opening, with no spider loot or kill reward. The first drawer reveals a one-time optional Project 08 record about prisoner tissue grafted to spider subjects and a demonic presence drawn into them. The record does not change progression gates or choose the final company name. [Supply placements](SupplyPlacements.md) records locations and the current limitation that optional inventory resets after app relaunch.

Only one enemy archetype is implemented and placed: the common flesh spider, using the recovered adult model, plus two juveniles activated during the existing encounter. The juveniles now use an imported CC0 animated Quaternius spider with idle, walk, attack, jump and death clips; a code-native visual remains the missing-asset fallback. [Common flesh-spider data](../Godot/data/common_flesh_spider.json) records adult **100 health and zero armor**, attack timing, movement, slight flashlight susceptibility, telegraph, and synthesized cues; it also records juvenile health and bite behavior. The adult's windup uses recovered motion with added front-leg, pedipalp and fang poses, procedural crouch/lean and a dim red pulse, followed by a lunge and retreat; the route still resolves by escaping. The imported juvenile attack clip and body compression precede its bite. Other resistance names in source are future parameter placeholders, not additional authored spider types.

The latest [house dressing pass](HouseDressing.md) suppresses 260 oversized or repeated non-plot props without deleting source meshes. It creates five web treatments: seven corner fans, five radial webs, five hanging veils, five ceiling sheets, and six furniture wraps. Thirty blood and black-goo decals now cover wall panels, static furniture, and floorboard edges. One 1K CC0 painted cabinet, worn shelf, and caged fixture mesh are placed; the fixture adds no new light. The imported furniture remains separate from the working code-native drawer and cabinet. The [web and voice pass](WebAndVoicePass.md) adds one local Luke text-to-speech placeholder near the webs. Its loaded-house integration test verified four conditions: the WAV loads, proximity fires once, the subtitle appears, and repeat activation is rejected. Mara's warning remains subtitled.
