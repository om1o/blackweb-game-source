# Web and voice pass

The new corner-web source image is `Godot/assets/generated/corner_web_v1.png` (1254 × 1254 RGBA, 1.77 MB). It was created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project and copied into the game source. Prompt: *A realistic, old triangular house-corner spiderweb, with layered radial and spiral silk, broken strands and dust, isolated as a transparent RGBA cutout for a 3D survival-horror scene; no wall, spider, text or watermark.* The image is a texture, not a 3D model; the house dressing script supplies geometry and must be checked in the rendered scene for transparency and overdraw.

The wall and furniture residue atlas is `Godot/assets/generated/blood_goo_decal_v1.png` (1774 × 887 RGBA, 1.43 MB). It was created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project; the left half is dried burgundy blood, and the right half is viscous black organic residue. Prompt: *Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent RGBA decal atlas to UV-map onto game walls and furniture. One square image split into exactly TWO independent decals: LEFT half a dark dried burgundy blood splash with thin drips and scattered droplets; RIGHT half a black viscous organic goo smear with hanging stringy drips and subtle wet highlights. Each decal must be centered in its half with generous fully transparent margin and must not cross the center line. The entire background is genuinely transparent (alpha zero), including inside gaps between droplets and drips. NO wall, paper, plaster, white/pink/gray backing, shadows, red glow/halo, yellow outlines, text, logos, body parts or frames. Straight-on orthographic texture view; physically plausible rough dried blood and glossy tar-like goo, restrained horror realism, clean alpha edges. Each half should work as a separate cropped game decal.* The renderer uses the two UV halves as transparent cards; the generated PNG is original Blackweb art, not a photograph of a surface or a 3D mesh.

The compact-house curtain texture is `Godot/assets/generated/ceiling_silk_v2.png` (1774 × 887 RGBA, 2.11 MB). It was generated with imagegen as a broad, photoreal aged spider-silk cutout with irregular torn holes, layered strands, dust and faint brown residue, with transparent background and without room geometry, spiders, text or perfect geometric webs. `house_dressing.gd` maps it over 112 high wall and ceiling cards; one imported texture is reused rather than enlarging the package with duplicates. Native review is required because image generation does not guarantee realistic 3D integration.

Luke's first spoken line after approaching the web and backup flashlight area is: “Mara... these webs were not here yesterday.” The active recording is `Godot/assets/generated/luke_web_reveal_neural_v2.wav` (174,044 bytes, 24 kHz mono PCM16, 3.625 s). It was generated locally on 2026-11-04 with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) through [kokoro-js](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/blob/main/kokoro.js/README.md), using the `am_michael` American male voice at 0.92 speed. The [ONNX conversion](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX) and runtime are Apache-2.0; the model weights and Node dependencies stay outside the game package. SHA-256 of the generated WAV is `FF44E58F149EAAD6EA0ADD5C5E1DCCC74A6112C2A4FB92FF20203202106517C4`. `soundscape.gd` plays it at most once when Luke reaches the web zone after Mara's message, and shows the same line as an accessible on-screen notice. The earlier Windows Mark recording remains in source as `luke_web_reveal_v1.wav` for comparison. The new audio imports and loads in Godot, has no clipped samples, and still needs a human listening review; it is synthetic placeholder voice, not a final actor performance.

The house pass keeps the game's no-score audio direction: recovered footsteps and presence/door cues, a quiet room hum, and one short spoken reaction. The visual and audio assets are included only in the local game build, not in the public project site.

The one-time voice cue passed `Godot/tests/web_voice_integration_tests.gd` against the loaded house: the WAV loaded, the proximity trigger played once, its subtitle appeared, and a repeated call was rejected. The final [isolated portable smoke](../Playable/portable-smoke.json) also passed **21/21** checks and loaded the 3.625-second voice stream from the PCK. These checks verify event wiring and packaging rather than final voice acting or an audible mix on the target machine.
