# Compact tutorial-house pass

The playable opening now uses a sealed five-room portion of the recovered ground-floor house, about 13.4 × 8.5 m (`Godot/scripts/tutorial_house.gd`). The source mansion stays on disk for later chapters; runtime culling removes distant rooms and their collision, and copies recovered brick wall pieces to close the tutorial shell. The single exterior exit remains at the original front. Four interior doors open with focus and E, for five functional doors total. The rear vault door has a scuffed steel facing and wheel on the same animated hinge; a native open/closed capture verifies its 1.46-radian swing.

The entry contains Mara's phone. The record room carries the keys and a company paper; the supply room has an early visible juvenile spider and supplies; the rear infested room carries the adult encounter; and the vault room holds the searchable cabinet, a cash prop and another juvenile. The cash is story dressing, not an invented currency mechanic. The encounter still begins at the existing progression stage, so a visible spider in the earlier room does not attack before the reveal.

Luke can draw one of three starter items with keys 1–3, use the drawn item with left click, and use F for the torch. The flashlight remains visibly held and drains its weak battery; the worn knife remains in his fist between attacks and slashes on click; the candy bar can be held and eaten. Crouch and jump apply authored skeletal poses to the recovered Luke rig, bending knees and changing leg position as well as moving the controller. These are functional in-game poses, not purchased motion-capture clips. See the input and equipment tests for behavior, and the local native `Evidence/player-*.png` captures for the current animation quality.

The shell keeps nearby practical lights, lowers powered ambient from 0.10 to 0.075, and uses the flashlight to reveal the dark rear. The current pass adds 95.03 MB of distinct surface source art, three modeled web styles, eight stain silhouettes and different dry/wet material responses across the existing 150 placements. The normal maps use GPU compression; runtime imports stay at or below 2K with mipmaps. [EnvironmentArtPass.md](EnvironmentArtPass.md) and [HandContactPass.md](HandContactPass.md) document the visual changes. These are still authored prototype visuals, not an AAA-quality certification.
