# Escape, story and motion pass — 7 November 2026

Luke returns from his maintenance shift to a webbed Greyhaven. Mica has put his exit keys with the evidence in the house vault. The front lock is fouled with silk. The playable objective now requires the brass vault key and hidden note, the actual exit-key pickup inside the vault, two knife cuts through the lock silk, and surviving the encounter before the front door opens. These milestones save independently and reset on New Game. The plant and folded code note remain outside the locked vault.

The house retains five rooms and one exterior exit. The rear wall moved one metre outward to give the vault and nest usable space. The top-right surveyed map reveals visited rooms and shows Luke, interior doors and exit readiness without revealing enemies or the hidden code. Inventory records now retain exit-key and lock status. Added Dad's last-shift letter and a containment memo explain the family connection and distinguish ordinary house spiders from the missing underground subjects.

Two additional ordinary juveniles activate on visible approach in the supply room and nest. They deactivate when the larger encounter starts, bounding simultaneous AI. Entry and encounter spiders remain. Juveniles have 12 HP and 4-damage bites; the worn knife deals 4 damage per successful timed strike. Bites now include a committed short physical dart and recovery. Hit feedback uses bounded pools: 64 droplet particles total, eight reusable floor marks, spatial impact audio and pale cut-silk fragments. No particle lights or shadows were added.

PINOC clips drive the recovered Luke rig through sampled rest-relative bone rotations, with the existing collision capsule and item grip remaining authoritative. Crouch changes body pose and capsule height; jump is driven by physics with skeletal leg motion; knife actions use a generated slash blended into and out of the held ready pose. Front-hit reaction is layered on the torso. The source X Bot mesh is excluded from the runtime. The animated intro now includes the licensed spider's bite pose, a lunge and Luke's sidestep/raised-arm response. It is a rendered 3D movie, not a still-image slideshow.

Vault clothing displays use the same fitted MakeHuman garments as the wearable kit. New aged-leather color detail retains the existing Poly Haven normal/roughness maps. A short-range, unshadowed case light improves pickup visibility. Looking down now shortens the shoulder camera before it enters the ceiling silk. The Meshy containment drum is reduced to about 4,500 triangles and 1K textures; its optimized GLB is 527,364 bytes versus the 39,567,252-byte supplied export.

## Validation and performance

The frozen package passed all 57 portable smoke checks. Native engine tests passed 30 escape-objective checks, 56 tutorial-journey checks, 16 knife-input checks, 38 phone/audio checks and 10 movie checks. The motion retargeting suite passed 10 checks, traversal passed 27 and impact effects passed 25. Native screenshots were inspected for the inventory, readable clue, garment display, body motion and cinematic attack. Desktop automation was unavailable, so these are engine-driven tests rather than a complete manual playthrough.

The final package SHA-256 is `d869092093ab5f8f7103ab77ebd4a1a5475271c084115290ba4fc0be677553e7`. Its nine-leg route completed through all five rooms with four armor pieces equipped, active spiders and 11 knife actions. On the Radeon RX 550, Performance preset, a 960×540 window and VSync, 2,899 measured frame intervals averaged 18.006 ms (about 55.5 FPS); p95 was 20.488 ms and p99 38.776 ms. The worst interval was **1,141.872 ms during the first camera pan**, an unresolved hitch. Frames over 50 ms were 0.586%. Loading warm-up took 5.432 seconds. Readback images measured 960×539; Godot's viewport getter reported 674×378, so this result must not be presented as a verified native-resolution GPU benchmark. No second Godot renderer was running. Results include the automation's overhead and normal desktop scheduling.

Warm-up now covers body clothing, held items and pooled hit effects without changing ownership or leaving active effects. It did not eliminate the first-pan hitch, and its precise cause remains unconfirmed. Full raw route evidence stays locally in `Evidence/EscapePass/route.json`; the compact, versioned result is `Docs/EscapeAndMotionValidation.json`. This pass does not establish AAA visual quality, zero stutter, or a 100× speedup.
