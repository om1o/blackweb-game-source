# Blackweb story direction

## Core premise

An unnamed company ran covert spider experiments using tissue taken from prisoners. Its researchers grafted human flesh into spider subjects. The work also drew demonic presences into the altered animals. These are story facts for the project, while the exact scientific and supernatural mechanism is deliberately left open for later chapters.

## House opening

Luke's immediate goal stays personal: hear Mara's warning, find his flashlight and car keys, survive the encounter, and leave the house to find her. A single optional company record hidden in a drawer or cabinet can suggest the larger mystery. The record should imply human tissue grafts and an unexplained presence without stopping the opening to explain the whole conspiracy. It must never be required for the exit.

The house should feel lived in and physically plausible. Keep ordinary, correctly scaled furnishings; remove giant or repeated placeholder props that do not serve gameplay or this story. Use webs, dust, damaged surfaces and a few believable medical or electrical objects as environmental evidence. Any conspicuous lab equipment belongs where the company has actually operated, rather than scattered through every room.

## Later phase trajectory

1. **House:** A strange attack interrupts Luke's search for Mara. He finds the first trace of the experiment.
2. **Greyhaven:** Missing prisoners and company supply routes connect the outbreak to nearby people and places.
3. **Facility:** Records reveal the grafting trials, their human cost, and how the company concealed failures.
4. **Breach:** The altered spiders and demonic presences threaten beyond the facility; Luke searches for Mara amid the spread.
5. **Resolution:** Luke confronts those responsible and chooses how to contain the breach and protect survivors.

These are direction markers, not claims that phases 2–5 are implemented. The company name, exact origin of the demons, Mara's role, and ending choices remain open for the user's creative decisions.

## Current interaction and inventory boundary

The phone, flashlight, keys, encounter and exit remain plot-critical. Searchable storage and the experiment record are optional. Three quick-item slots are planned, with the flashlight reserved as one slot. The other two items and external asset packs are pending the user's selection; a HUD slot does not imply a weapon is already present or usable.
