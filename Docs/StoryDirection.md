# Blackweb story direction

## Core premise

An unnamed company ran covert spider experiments using tissue taken from prisoners. Its researchers grafted human flesh into spider subjects. The work also drew demonic presences into the altered animals. These are story facts for the project, while the exact scientific and supernatural mechanism is deliberately left open for later chapters.

## House opening

Luke's immediate goal stays personal: hear Mica's warning, recover the exit keys from the vault, survive the encounter, cut the silk covering the front lock, and leave the house to find her. He starts with a weak flashlight, worn knife and candy bar. Mica's interrupted plant clue leads to the hidden code note outside the vault. Optional company records suggest the larger mystery without stopping the opening to explain the whole conspiracy.

The house should feel lived in and physically plausible. Keep ordinary, correctly scaled furnishings; remove giant or repeated placeholder props that do not serve gameplay or this story. Use webs, dust, damaged surfaces and a few believable medical or electrical objects as environmental evidence. Any conspicuous lab equipment belongs where the company has actually operated, rather than scattered through every room.

## Later phase trajectory

1. **House:** A strange attack interrupts Luke's search for Mica. He finds the first trace of the experiment.
2. **Greyhaven:** Missing prisoners and company supply routes connect the outbreak to nearby people and places. A proposed first major boss encounter leads to Luke's capture near the greenhouse.
3. **Facility:** Luke wakes in the company greenhouse laboratory. Records reveal the grafting trials, their human cost, and how the company concealed failures.
4. **Breach:** The altered spiders and demonic presences threaten beyond the facility; Luke searches for Mica amid the spread.
5. **Resolution:** Luke confronts those responsible and chooses how to contain the breach and protect survivors.

These are direction markers, not claims that phases 2–5 are implemented. The company name, exact origin of the demons, Mica's role, and ending choices remain open for the user's creative decisions. The [first boss proposal](FirstBossPlan.md) develops the greenhouse transition; its adult-subject creature and encounter details await creative review.

## Current interaction and inventory boundary

The phone, spare cells, vault key, hidden note, exit keys, encounter and exit remain plot-critical. Searchable storage and additional experiment records are optional. The implemented inventory has 50 cells, three assignable quick slots and four separate armour slots. Starting items are flashlight, worn knife and candy bar; batteries, bandages and flares are findable. The four leather pieces and persistent cash are in the vault. The current house enemies are ordinary spiders; the human-spider experiments belong to later chapters.
