# Ordinary tutorial spiders and contact feedback

The tutorial's adult and juvenile enemies are ordinary house spiders. Their existing game balance remains 100 adult HP, 12 juvenile HP, 35 lunge damage, 18 adult bite damage and 4 juvenile bite damage in the house. The legacy `common_flesh_spider.json` filename and ID remain compatible; its display name and status now describe house spiders.

`combat_effects.gd` supplies one shared pool per world: four one-shot emitters of eight particles each, with a 0.34-second particle lifetime and 30 Hz particle simulation. Tiny muted ochre specks mark accepted weapon hits/death; subdued dust marks accepted bite contact. Four pooled mono voices play original synthesized dry transients at those contact positions. Materials have no emission, shadows or textures; there are no effect lights, decals, particle collisions or downloaded effect assets. Full pools reuse the oldest slot, and idle script processing is disabled. Pause clears the pool; activation/deactivation/checkpoint reset cancels that enemy's effects.

The old adult attack-light node remains hidden with zero energy for compatibility with earlier QA tools. Posture, fang movement and dry audio still telegraph attacks. Existing single-hit attack rules, dodges, line of sight, hit reactions and death state handling remain in place.

Weapon contact now uses `get_hurt_spheres()` and `receive_hit(amount, world_point, world_normal)`. Adult spheres follow the recovered rig's Root and Back bones with radii 0.22 m and 0.25 m. Juvenile spheres follow the corrected Literock head and abdomen midpoint with radii 0.095 m and 0.12 m. These approximate the visible body sections; thin legs are not inflated into large hurt volumes. Existing movement capsules remain unchanged, preserving door and navigation clearance. Dormant and dead spiders expose no hurt volumes.

`tutorial_props.gd` adds two original lightweight placeholder factories. `make_plant()` returns a floor-origin terracotta pot, soil, three stems and sixteen curved leaf blades with raised, lighter central veins in one foliage MultiMesh. `make_leather_kit()` returns folded shirt/pants, a hood and paired shoes with seams and buckles on a 0.75 × 0.50 m case. Its origin is the centre of the case top plane; the case extends 0.12 m downward. These are code-authored placeholder meshes, not scanned or final production art. Both factories disable mesh shadows and add no collision, scripts, external textures or per-frame processing.

Validation: 25 effects/hurt-volume checks, 31 juvenile combat checks, 22 creature visual checks, 9 bite-occlusion checks, 12 pursuit checks, 63 adult combat checks and 18 existing adult integration checks passed headlessly. Particle readability and prop appearance require native visual review; these checks do not establish rendered performance.

Implementation references: Godot's [CPUParticles3D documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_cpuparticles3d.html) for bounded one-shot world-space particles, and [PhysicsShapeQueryParameters3D documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_physicsshapequeryparameters3d.html) for separating query volumes from movement collision. Body positions were measured from the local imported skeletons; no third-party assets were acquired for this pass.
