# House prop and web pass

The playable Godot house applies `Godot/scripts/house_dressing.gd` after loading the recovered scene and before occlusion setup and static batching. The pass removes **260** source props from the active scene. It does not delete the recovered source meshes or edit the recovery manifest, so their provenance and original placement remain available.

| Recovered prop | Removed | Evidence from `Godot/data/house_import_report.json` |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Plate | 180 | Ten towers of 18 plates; each plate is about 1.33 m across. |
| Loose books and book rows | 12 | Loose books span about 1.14–3.07 m; rows span about 3.2–4.2 m. |
| Cassettes and poker decks | 20 | Cassettes span about 4.3 m; card decks about 2.6 m. |
| Toothbrushes, toilet brushes, skimmers, soap and cooking pots | 35 | Everyday handheld objects measure about 1.1–10 m in their recovered placement. |
| Trays, breadbox and trash cans | 13 | Trays span about 2.2–2.4 m, the breadbox about 1.6 m, and trash cans up to about 3 m. |

These props do not carry the opening's phone, keys, flashlight, exit door, spider, or progression logic. Furniture, structural geometry, ordinary room clutter, and the original lighting fixtures remain. The cleanup removes visual scale errors and some collision from oversized books; it does not claim a measured FPS improvement.

The pass now stages several visibly different silk treatments through the compact five-room house: seven high wall-corner strand fans (518 triangles), five incomplete radial webs (2,128 triangles), five dangling veils (870 triangles), five larger sheet bridges hanging from wall/ceiling joins, and six localized cross-wraps on static furniture. A second generated texture supplies 112 aged silk curtain cards (224 triangles) on the perimeter, central spine, both faces of the new cross walls, and above the otherwise bare ceiling. The furniture wraps avoid functional search units. The original corner-web texture supplies fourteen crossed alpha-cutout cards at seven wall corners, five sheet cards and six furniture-wrap cards. Procedural strands add 154 triangles to the sheets and wraps. All web geometry together is about 3,919 triangles across seven mesh surfaces. Alpha scissor removes low-alpha haze. The cards were tinted warmer and darker after native review, though their flat silhouettes remain visible at close range. Sheets and veils stay high against walls; all webs have no collision, cast no shadows, and require no new light. No Resident Evil art or animation was copied.

A separate transparent atlas, `Godot/assets/generated/blood_goo_decal_v1.png`, supplies dark dried blood on its left half and black arachnoid residue on its right. The current 150 small, varied alpha-cutout cards (300 triangles) dress perimeter and partition brick, the static painted cabinet and shelf, and floorboard edges. They share one mesh surface and use normals so Luke's flashlight reveals their red and black texture. The texture has a deterministic procedural fallback if its imported source is unavailable. None of the decals carries collision, casts shadows, creates lights, or obscures an interactive search fixture. The total added web and residue dressing is about 4,219 triangles across eight surfaces. The texture is reused instead of padding the game with a large duplicate decal file; visible coverage and frame time are the relevant targets.

One weathered [Poly Haven Painted Wooden Cabinet](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_wooden_cabinet) and one [worn shelf](https://polyhaven.com/a/Shelf_01) are placed against sparse walls with simple box colliders. Their furniture-wrap webs do not block any interactive drawer or pickup. A single [caged hanging light mesh](https://polyhaven.com/a/caged_hanging_light) hangs under an existing recovered light position; it adds no `Light3D`. All three use imported 1K [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) glTF. The cabinet's separate doors and drawers remain static background art; the code-native EntryDrawer and HallCabinet remain the working opening fixtures. See [ThirdPartyAssets.md](ThirdPartyAssets.md) for exact source files and other candidates.

`Godot/tests/house_dressing_tests.gd` loads the actual recovered scene and checks all counts, card materials, high placement of the new meshes, disabled web shadows and collisions, furniture colliders and the fixture's lack of a light. It passed with Godot 4.7.2 in headless mode at 150 decals and 112 curtain cards. The dummy headless renderer emits a null-material teardown message for the original recovered scene even without this dressing pass; it is not a test failure. Native rendered review on Intel HD Graphics 530 is saved locally as `Evidence/tutorial-*.png`, `Evidence/web-expansion-*.png` and `Evidence/residue-*.png`. The blood and goo are visible under the flashlight; the still-flat web cutouts and repeated decal forms remain prototype art. These images have not been cleared for public hosting.

The same-process 1280×720 cabinet-view [frame-time trial](../Evidence/web-residue-frame-trial.json) sampled 120 frames for each bare/dressed/bare phase. Median frame time was 30.773 / 33.517 / 31.253 ms, while draw calls were 49 / 56 / 49. The corresponding p95 values varied 55.711 / 51.306 / 63.922 ms. This establishes a local median cost of roughly 2.3–2.7 ms for the entire web and residue pass on this Intel HD 530/ANGLE view, not a whole-route FPS guarantee or a measured improvement.
