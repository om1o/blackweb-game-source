Adaptations for Blackweb: geometry fitted and reweighted to the existing Luke
rig; sleeve and torso fit adjusted; covered original clothing masked; larger
source textures resized to 2048; material changed to brown leather. The jacket,
pants and hood were originally clothing rather than leather armor. Original
UVs, seams and selected normal detail remain. These changes are not endorsed
by the original artists.

Brown Leather PBR material by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven, CC0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather
https://polyhaven.com/license

Luke's existing rest rig and transferred skin weights are derived from
Microsoft Rocketbox Male_Adult_04, under MIT; see the bundled Rocketbox notice.

Detailed source URLs, file hashes and modifications are recorded in
assets/third_party/makehuman_armor/source-manifest.json and ATTRIBUTION.md.
