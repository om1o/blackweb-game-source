# Arachnophobia: Blackweb — house opening

**GitHub source checkout:** This private repository contains the authored Godot game code, tests, tools, documentation, and redistributable third-party/generated art. It intentionally omits the supplied original game's recovered house/spider assets, generated scenes containing those meshes, the portable executable/PCK, local saves, and screenshots. A clone is therefore not a standalone playable download. The playable links and test evidence below refer to the complete local workspace; see [source checkout requirements](Docs/SourceCheckout.md).

The active project is [Godot/project.godot](Godot/project.godot), running with the bundled **Godot 4.7.2** portable runtime. No Unreal Editor is required. The user's later instruction authorized rebuilding from the supplied assets without Unreal; earlier C++ scaffolding and Unreal files remain as prior work and are not the active build.

The recovered house is the playable tutorial opening of the full **Arachnophobia: Blackweb** game. Later chapters and final production art are still in development. The active route is a sealed five-room section, about 13.4 × 8.5 m, with four opening interior doors and one exterior exit. The entry and record room carry two lore papers; the supply, infested and vault rooms carry the early sighting or later spiders. The vault has a hinged steel-faced door, a searchable cabinet and unmarked cash. Mara's warning, keys, backup flashlight, blackout, spider encounter and the exit route are implemented. Luke starts with a working 22% flashlight, a worn knife and a candy bar; batteries, bandages and flares are discoverable in the house and searchable storage. The user's chosen **Minimal** HUD is the default of four layouts. `I` opens a 50-space inventory with three active quick slots; found items must be equipped there before use. The flashlight drains and flickers at low charge. One common flesh-spider archetype is placed as an adult with two juveniles that join during the encounter. The flashlight has a slight slowing effect on them; escape, not killing, remains the route objective. [TutorialHousePass.md](Docs/TutorialHousePass.md) and [GameplayMilestone.md](Docs/GameplayMilestone.md) record the behavior and checks.

The house pass suppresses 260 implausibly scaled or repeated non-plot props while retaining their recovered source. It adds several silk treatments including 112 aged curtain cards, 150 blood and black-goo decals, darker practical lighting, one static CC0 painted cabinet, a worn shelf and a caged fixture mesh. Luke carries a 1K CC0 Poly Haven flashlight model, a weathered CC0 knife and original Blender candy and supply models. His drawn knife stays fixed in his fist through a windup, diagonal slash and recovery, dealing four base damage on a timed mouse click; crouch and jump bend and move the recovered rig's legs. Battery replacement, bandaging and flare ignition now use delayed effects and held-item poses; see [SupplyActionPass.md](Docs/SupplyActionPass.md). These are authored prototype poses, not motion-capture clips. The juvenile spiders use an optimized 1K Literock rig in the local game package and a CC0 source-checkout fallback. Mara's phone warning, Luke's reply and his later web reaction use local synthetic voices with subtitles; [PhoneVoicePass.md](Docs/PhoneVoicePass.md) records the exact lines and timing. [HouseDressing.md](Docs/HouseDressing.md), [LiterockSpider.md](Docs/LiterockSpider.md), and [ThirdPartyAssets.md](Docs/ThirdPartyAssets.md) give asset details.

The latest Intel HD Graphics 530 native five-room workload at 960 × 540 recorded 2,080 rendered frames over 48.4 seconds: 21.97 ms median, 33.07 ms p95 and 23.27 ms mean (about 43 FPS). It included active spiders, real controller movement and eleven knife actions, with no second game process running. The vault was slowest at 40.39 ms p95, and a 590 ms early hitch remains in the results. This is a source-build profiling workload, not a human playtest, a guarantee of stable 30 FPS, or a 100× improvement. See [WholeRoutePerformance.md](Docs/WholeRoutePerformance.md) for the full method. The earlier entrance-only measurements remain in [Phase1RenderCheck.md](Docs/Phase1RenderCheck.md).

That workload exposed a table and sofa blocking the south-room doorway for Luke's full collision capsule. Both props now leave standing-body clearance. The new traversal regression fails four checks on the preceding package and passes all twenty after the fix; the real controller also completes the full five-room route. Earlier narrative-only route tests had not visited both optional south rooms.

```powershell

```

Controls are WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to sprint, `C` to crouch, Space to jump, and `E` to interact. Keys `1`–`3` draw the corresponding equipped item; left click uses it. The starter loadout is flashlight, worn knife and candy bar. `F` toggles the flashlight while it is equipped. `I` opens the inventory: click a stored item, then one of the three quick slots to equip it. `F1`–`F4` choose a HUD, and Esc pauses or closes inventory. Opening storage follows `Open → Search → Close` with `E`. Checkpoints use separate BlackwebHouseRebuild user data; the original game's saves are not reused. Optional supply counts and taken pickups persist through an in-session fatal retry but reset after app relaunch. No microphone capture is requested.

The opening follows Mara's interrupted Greyhaven warning, keys/backup-flashlight preparation, a presence beat, power loss, a physical spider encounter and an opening-door transition. The optional Project 08 drawer record hints that an unnamed company grafted prisoner tissue to spiders and drew demonic presences into them; it does not gate the route. The adult common flesh spider has 100 health and zero armor, with two juvenile spiders. Other type names in source are future parameter placeholders, not separate finished species. The current item models, procedural poses and synthesized action cues are source prototypes; native held-item and action-pose captures were reviewed. Pickup/door contact, authored hand and locomotion clips, final spider leg grounding, actor voice and material fidelity remain unfinished.
